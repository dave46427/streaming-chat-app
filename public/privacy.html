DavaBridge Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

DavaBridge is a multi-platform streaming chat service designed to help users connect supported streaming platforms and bring live community interactions together in one place.

This Privacy Policy explains the types of information that DavaBridge may receive or process, why that information may be used, and the choices available to users.

1. Information DavaBridge May Process

When a user chooses to connect a supported third-party streaming platform, DavaBridge may process information made available by that platform and authorised by the user.

Depending on the connected platform and permissions granted, this may include:

  • Account or platform identifiers
  • Profile or display name information
  • Live stream information
  • Live chat messages and related interaction information
  • Information required to connect supported platform services

2. How Information Is Used

Information received from supported platforms is used to provide DavaBridge features requested by the user. This can include connecting supported streaming services and displaying supported live chat and community interactions within DavaBridge.

3. Facebook and Meta Services

If a user chooses to connect DavaBridge to a Facebook or Meta service, DavaBridge may receive information authorised by the user and made available through the relevant Meta services and APIs.

DavaBridge uses this information to provide the connection and streaming-chat functionality requested by the user.

4. Other Third-Party Services

DavaBridge may connect to supported third-party streaming platforms. Those services operate under their own terms, privacy policies and account settings.

Users should review the privacy information provided by each connected service to understand how that service handles information.

5. Data Security

DavaBridge is designed with security in mind. Reasonable measures are used to protect information processed while providing the service.

Users are also responsible for protecting their own streaming-platform accounts, passwords and other account credentials.

6. User Choices and Connected Accounts

Users choose which supported streaming platforms they connect to DavaBridge.

Users may disconnect services they no longer wish to use and may also manage application permissions using the settings provided by the relevant third-party platform.

7. Data Deletion

Users who want information associated with DavaBridge deleted can follow the instructions on the DavaBridge Data Deletion page.

View Data Deletion Instructions

8. Children

DavaBridge is intended for users who meet the applicable age requirements for the streaming platforms and services they choose to connect.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as DavaBridge develops, its features change, or its supported services change.

When the policy is updated, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be changed.

10. Contact

DavaBridge is currently under development. Additional support and contact information will be provided as the service moves toward public release.
