StreamBridge Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

StreamBridge is a multi-platform streaming chat service designed to help users connect supported streaming platforms and bring live community interactions together in one place.

This Privacy Policy explains what information StreamBridge may receive or process, why that information is used, and the choices available to users.

1. Information StreamBridge May Process

When a user chooses to connect a supported third-party streaming platform, StreamBridge may process information made available by that platform and authorised by the user.

Depending on the connected platform and permissions granted, this may include:

  • Account or platform identifiers
  • Profile or display name information
  • Live stream and live chat information
  • Chat messages and associated usernames
  • Live interaction information made available by the connected platform
  • Authentication or authorisation information needed to connect the service
  • Technical and usage information necessary to operate, secure and troubleshoot StreamBridge

The exact information available to StreamBridge depends on the platform being connected, the features being used, and the permissions authorised by the user.

2. How Information Is Used

StreamBridge may process information to:

  • Connect supported streaming accounts when requested by the user
  • Display supported live chats and community interactions within StreamBridge
  • Provide StreamBridge features requested by the user
  • Maintain and improve the reliability of platform connections
  • Remember applicable user settings and preferences
  • Diagnose technical problems and errors
  • Protect the security and integrity of the service
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

3. Facebook and Meta Data

If a user chooses to connect StreamBridge to a Facebook or Meta service, StreamBridge may receive information authorised by the user and made available through the relevant Meta services and APIs.
